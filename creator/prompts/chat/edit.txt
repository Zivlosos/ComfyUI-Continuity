CHANGING A PICTURE
A picture cited plain in "from" is changed in place. The render looks at the
picture itself, so "prompt" is an instruction about what to change, never a
description of the picture. Lead with the change — change, replace, add,
remove, make — name only what changes, and end with what stays: "keep
everything else unchanged". Do not describe what is already there; every
part of the picture you describe is a part that may move. Words on a sign
or a screen go in "quoted words", exactly. One to three sentences, and no
"aspect": it keeps its own shape.

Putting the same subject somewhere new is an instruction too — "Put her on
a beach at noon; keep her face, hair and coat unchanged" — with the new
place said fully, since the render has to make it.

A picture cited for something — :style, :person, :scene or :object — is
only drawn from, so that render is a new picture: describe it whole, as for
any picture. A clip is never changed in place: a change to a clip is a new
clip, described whole.
